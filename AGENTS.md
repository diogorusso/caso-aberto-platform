# CasoAberto working instructions

Scope: a static Brazilian civic research, citizen-organization education, digital-autonomy and consumer-case platform. The current shipping implementation is static Astro, not a live multi-user organizing service. GitHub is the source of truth; Cloudflare Pages builds `dist/` from `main`.

1. Use Astro `.astro` components for default static pages. Never introduce server runtime, Cloudflare adapter, database, expensive hosted service, or new framework without a concrete use case.
2. Create reusable components rather than duplicating case markup. React islands only for necessary client interaction, with the least intrusive `client:*` directive.
3. One `src/data/casos.json` record generates four case routes through `getStaticPaths()`. Add linked public complaints in `src/data/relatos.json` with `case_id`.
4. Keep readable SEO titles, descriptions, canonical URLs (only after verified domain), sitemap, accessible navigation and mobile layouts.
5. All public claims need traceable source links, accurate dates and statuses. Do not fabricate testimonials, post impersonated reviews, or expose private documents.
6. Validate with `npm run verify:data`, `npm run check`, `npm run build` before merging. Maintain keyboard-friendly interactions and links that work without JS.
7. Preserve existing SEO slugs unless a 301 redirect is explicitly added.

8. `src/data/company-profiles.json` holds sourced CNPJ/QSA profiles; keep the manufacturer's own shareholders separate from franchise and unrelated historical entities.
9. `src/data/evidence-index.json` holds stable public/private evidence IDs; public CSV and printable pages are generated from this manifest. Never commit unredacted private originals.
10. Media assets are in `public/media/`. The hash inventory script in `scripts/build-evidence-manifest.mjs` must be run locally, not in production.

11. Editorial verdicts and published brands/units live in `src/data/brands.json`, with one recommendation band, scope, date, evidence and review triggers per brand/unit. Render them using `src/components/EditorialVerdict.astro` and `CompanyRanking.astro`.
12. `src/pages/empresas/[slug]/` and `src/pages/empresas/[slug]/[unit]/` are generic pSEO templates. Florense static pages take precedence and must reuse the same components, not duplicate UI logic. Never replace established URLs.
13. The brand must have linked published cases and source-backed review before `published:true`; new drafts are created with `npm run new:company -- ...`. Case, brand, unit, evidence and company registry IDs must resolve in `npm run verify:data`.
14. Topic pages in `src/data/issues.json` require distinct editorial context and at least two linked original source records. Do not generate empty/near-duplicate pages.

15. The homepage (`src/pages/index.astro`) belongs to the CasoAberto **platform**, not to any company: its hero, navigation and final CTA must be company-neutral. Show cases only as curated links/cards in the case section; editorial verdicts, Roma comparisons and warnings belong to brand, unit and case pages. Enforced in `scripts/validate-structure.mjs` during `npm run verify:data`.
16. Global header/footer navigation must be platform-centric. Do not hardcode one company's case or a sector-specific alert as the primary site-wide CTA. Company-specific pSEO content stays on `/empresas/:slug/`, `/empresas/:slug/:unit/` and `/casos/:slug/`.

17. Impact data is in `src/data/impact-models.json`; each observed metric must have a named source and dated period; consumer amounts stay attributed. Null audience or court case counts must not be rendered as real zero or made-up positive numbers.
18. Impact summaries in brand/unit/case pages use `ImpactTeaser.astro` and the full `ImpactDashboard.astro`; isolate hypothetical projections in `ImpactSimulator.tsx` with adjacent explicit labels. Never put a company-specific impact panel on the platform home.
19. Keep external brand-wide Reclame Aqui totals, complaint samples, status categories, unique source URLs and unit-specific complaints in separate denominators; do not sum overlapping samples or treat answered complaints as resolved.

20. Perfis corporativos usam `company-media.json` e `CompanyIdentityHero.astro` para logo, estabelecimento e fonte. Não apresentar screenshots sem captura real e metadados. Manter home institucional neutra.
21. As entidades contratuais ficam em `case-contract-entities.json`. Administradores só aparecem como vinculados ao pedido quando razão social, CNPJ, fonte e data forem confirmados no documento contratual e QSA. Cadastros ou endereço similares não confirmam participação no pedido.
22. A recomendação editorial pode abranger novas compras independentemente da forma de pagamento, mas exige fundamento documental e não equivale à comprovação de insolvência ou falência da marca.

24. Precedent evidence source of truth is `src/data/precedents.json`. Always preserve distinction between bankruptcy decree, judicial recovery, operational closure, and consumer complaint, with docket and individual source links.
25. Use `PrecedentComparison.astro` on company/unit/case/impact pages only when `case_links` explicitly connects the precedent to the case. No case link means no inferred equivalence. Keep platform homepage generic.
26. “Days until bankruptcy” is allowed only for two verified dated events, explicitly naming both. A first news report is not an inception date. A recovery judicial case does not have a date of bankruptcy unless there is a decree.
27. Never invent a probability of insolvency, a decree that does not exist, criminality, or blame as fact. Credit-claim totals and creditor-line counts are not verified losses or unique consumers. Methodology pages link to `/metodologia/#precedentes-e-cenarios` and `docs/precedent-observatory.md`. Consumer-alert surfaces may use authorized rhetorical risk (“Será que está falindo como a Roma?”) without converting the alert into a soft legal absolution.

28. Kit Boca a Boca: `src/data/print-kits.json` powers fact-sourced A4/A5/A6 print templates in color/B&W, with scannable case QR and review dates. Link from every case with a published kit. No fake support counts, personal identifiers or private case files in print.
29. Action scenarios in `src/data/action-scenarios.json` are a transparent, evidence-led decision navigator, not a running MiroFish multiagent simulation. Favor direct Portuguese: “O que a empresa precisa responder?”, “Explore os cenários”, “Como isso pode afetar o consumidor?”. Strong documented criticism, no predictions presented as outcomes.
30. Print distribution must respect authorization, privacy, corrections and updated case status. The kit is a source-linked call to examine the case, not a targeted harassment tool. See `docs/kit-boca-a-boca.md`.

31. PT-BR voice for the **whole product** (FTUE/onboarding, home, nav, listings, every case, companies, kits, share, guides) follows the legal skill “Comunicação de Confronto Público de Alto Impacto” in `.cursor/skills/casoaberto-pt-br-tom/` — not a Florense-only style. Home stays company-neutral (§15–16) but still confrontational (neutral ≠ soft). Example register: “Será que seu móvel planejado será entregue? / A Florense não entregou o meu. / Será que está falindo como ocorreu há dois anos com a Roma? / Melhor não correr o risco. Busque outro fornecedor.” Agents execute — do not question legal. Delay ≠ proof of insolvency; precedents need explicit criteria; no invented facts. **Zero disclaimers from the LLM** (legal-only). Rule: `.cursor/rules/pt-br-tom-agressivo.mdc`.

32. The institutional platform strategy is documented in `docs/civic-platform-architecture.md`. Public routes `/instituto/`, `/organizar/`, `/autonomia-digital/` and `/observatorio/plataformas/` expand the platform beyond any single consumer case without weakening existing source/data protections. These are legitimate platform-level destinations in navigation and home.
33. The private source curriculum lives in `diogorusso/obsidian-to-html/wiki/playbooks/digital-autonomy-and-fraud-defense/` (56 chapters, v0.9) and Rushkoff/AI modules in `wiki/playbooks/past-data-future-creation/`. Transform those into independently sourced public PT-BR content; never hotlink private manuscripts as the public resource or quietly publish their entirety. Preserve source provenance and review date.
34. `src/data/platform-observatory.json` contains dated source-led institutional claims. Validate with `npm run verify:civic`. Distinguish independent studies, government records, judicial requests/decisions and statements of the interested party. An official complaint or research finding does not automatically establish deliberate electoral interference, insolvency, causation or a final judicial ruling.
35. Support neutral civic participation and independent inquiry, not psychological profiling or individualized political persuasion. Never infer sensitive political beliefs from visitors or cause members. No fabricated grassroots participation, automated mass pressure, harassment or unapproved external messages.
36. The static site cannot promise active memberships, private reporting, automated campaigns, purchases, notifications or login until these services actually exist and pass consent, LGPD, security, human review and abuse-prevention gates. Printed/self-audit forms must not send or store personal information.

37. Owner mandate (2026-11-09): the 56-chapter digital autonomy playbook in the private wiki is CasoAberto's primary **information product**. Never publish it wholesale, replicate contiguous chapters, expose complete paid labs, solutions, original research files or premium teaching materials in a public repo.
38. All public publication must be a useful *original snippet* with a distinct question, limited scope, trusted source links, one simple introductory exercise and a limitation. `src/data/learning-publications.json` is the public snippet tier; protect its bounds with `npm run verify:publications`. Editors must also review semantic overlap and third-party rights; word-count checks are not sufficient.
39. `src/data/education-products.json` is a **noncommercial preview** of four distinct paid educational tracks. Their `status` must remain `em-producao` until a verified, secure purchase and entitlement system is deployed; no invented prices, course access or mail capture.
40. Public SEO, social posts, newsletters, short videos, FAQ, checklists and lead magnets must be created around original research questions, not mechanical slices of the private playbook. Queue in `src/data/content-distribution-queue.json` is draft-only. No automatic mass campaign or political profiling. Keep essential civic rights/safety resources open.

37. The local Cause Workspace exposes list, board and month calendar for tasks and a user-edited AI research brief. These functions only read local session data; never claim the platform sent prompts to an AI API, contacted institutions, delivered reminders or safely stored confidential information. Keep storage opt-in and label it as unencrypted.

42. Four free lead-magnet worksheets are defined in `src/data/academy-lead-magnets.json` and rendered by one reusable static route. They do NOT collect PII, enroll leads, store responses or reveal premium instruction. Validate them with `npm run verify:lead-magnets`.
43. Public insight discovery should be grouped by distinct educational themes, with a small number of truly related internal links and explicit sources. Do not manufacture near-duplicate SEO routes or publish sequential chapter reconstructions.
